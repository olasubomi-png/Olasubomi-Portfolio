Who I Am

I'm Olasubomi — a passionate full-stack developer with a deep love for automation, AI integration, and cloud infrastructure.

My journey started with curiosity — wondering how apps work behind the scenes. That curiosity grew into building production-ready WhatsApp bots with hundreds of features, deploying them on AWS EC2, managing them with PM2 and Nginx, and integrating AI APIs to create truly intelligent experiences.
