About

BUILDING FROM IDEA TO PRODUCTION

I'm Ola Subomi, a full-stack developer and software engineer focused on building useful software from the ground up.

My work includes multi-tenant SaaS platforms, commerce systems, communication products, streaming catalogs, and automation tooling. The goal is always the same: ship software that is structured, maintainable and deployable.
